FAQ

Quick answers

From what age can my child join?

From 4 years old. Sessions are grouped by age and level, and our coaches specialize in first-timers.

Do we need to bring skates or protection?

No — all gear (skates/boards, helmets, pads) is included in every training session.

How long is a session?

60 minutes of training plus 15 minutes of free play. Training runs daily from 4 to 11 PM.

What if we miss a session in the monthly package?

Missed sessions in monthly subscriptions can't be made up, so pick a weekly slot that works for you.

How do I pay?

Book online to reserve your spot, then pay at the park before your session.
