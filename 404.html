Facts for parents

Confidence starts before the first push.

The exciting part belongs to the rider. The clear information belongs to you.
01

Protection is part of training

Boards or skates, helmets and pads are included in training sessions.

02

Small-group attention

The supplied program information lists 5 certified coaches and groups of up to 4 riders, matched by age and level.

03

Safety is taught, not assumed

Tell the team about injuries before training. First-timers are taught how to fall safely before progression.

04

A session has room to learn and enjoy

60 minutes of training is followed by 15 minutes of free play.

05

You can stay close

Parents can settle into the park-side family seating and Egy Bar area.

Quick answers

Know before you roll.

From what age can my child join?+

From age 4. Sessions are matched by age and level, with first-timers welcome.

Do we bring skates, boards or protection?+

The supplied training information says boards or skates, helmets and pads are included in training sessions. Confirm your rider’s size when the team replies.

How long is training?+

The listed format is 60 minutes of training plus 15 minutes of free play.

What happens if we miss a monthly session?+

The supplied package policy says missed monthly sessions cannot be made up, so agree a weekly time that works before confirming.

How is the final time confirmed?+

The website creates a request. The team confirms the suitable coach, group, exact time and current details on WhatsApp.
