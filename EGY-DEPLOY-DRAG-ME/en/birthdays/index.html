A celebration that moves

Turn the birthday into the story they keep telling.

First-timers and experienced riders can share a coached skate experience with equipment and protection options. The real concrete park, evening light and movement create photographs and memories that feel completely different from a standard party venue.

Plan the right format

Send the date, number of riders and their ages.

Birthday and group formats depend on group size and the experience you want. Message the team with the preferred date, estimated number of children, their ages and whether you need coaching, equipment, drinks or desserts.

Questions before your visit.

Can complete beginners join?+

Yes. Tell the team how many riders are first-timers so coaching and equipment can be planned correctly.

Are school visits available?+

Yes. The team accepts enquiries for birthdays, schools, communities and group skate days.

How do I get a price?+

Send the date, group size, ages and requested format on WhatsApp for a current plan and quotation.
