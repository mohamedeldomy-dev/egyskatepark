BEFORE YOU ROLL

Everything worth knowing.

01Is the park suitable for complete beginners?+

Yes. First-timers are welcome, and coached training begins from age 4.

02Do I need my own equipment?+

No. Training equipment and protection are included in every class price. If you are visiting just to ride, skateboards, roller skates, helmets and full protection are available to rent at the park.

03Can parents stay during the visit?+

Yes. Family seating keeps the park in view, with drinks and desserts available from Egy Bar.

04How is a class confirmed?+

Send the short request below. The team confirms the coach, level, date, time and current price on WhatsApp.

05Are padel and skatepark entry one ticket?+

No. Padel is booked and charged separately from skatepark entry.
