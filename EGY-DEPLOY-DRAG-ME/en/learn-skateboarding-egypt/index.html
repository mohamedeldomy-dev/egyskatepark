Learn on a real park

Start with control. Build confidence. Find your style.

Egy Skatepark gives skateboard riders real bowls, banks and street lines instead of a flat practice floor. Beginners start with stance, pushing, stopping and safe falling, then progress into turns, pumping and connected lines.

  • First-timers welcome
  • Board, helmet and protection included in classes
  • Real concrete European design
  • One dedicated skateboard coach
How skateboard booking works

Agree the time with the coach first.

Choose a preferred hour or the flexible-time option in the booking request. The team confirms the coach and final time on WhatsApp. Riders who do not need coaching can use the 150 EGP day pass and rent a complete setup.
