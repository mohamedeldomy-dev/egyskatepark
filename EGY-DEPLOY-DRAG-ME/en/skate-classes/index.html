A confident first session

Learn the safe foundations before chasing tricks.

Egy Skatepark welcomes first-timers from age four as well as riders ready to progress. Coaches begin with stance, balance, stopping and how to fall safely, then build the session around the rider's age, confidence and current level.

  • 60 minutes of coached training
  • 15 minutes of free play
  • Around four riders in a matched group
  • Board or skates, helmet and full protection included
Choose your training rhythm

One clear price. No equipment purchase required.

Start with a private session for 300 EGP, join the 650 EGP monthly group path, choose a 750 EGP monthly private group for up to three riders, or book the 1,000 EGP monthly one-to-one path. Monthly paths include one session each week.

Questions before your visit.

What is the minimum age?+

Children can begin from age four. The team matches the rider with a suitable coach, group and level.

Do we need to buy equipment?+

No. Every class price includes the training board or roller skates, helmet and full protection.

How is the time confirmed?+

Send the booking request online. The team confirms the suitable coach and exact hour on WhatsApp.

Your next step

Choose the rider, activity and training path. Send the request, then let the team confirm the suitable available coach and time.
