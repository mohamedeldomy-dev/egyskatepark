How can I improve my child's balance?

Balance is a skill developed through calm practice—not a verdict on a child's courage or ability.

What age can children start balance activities?

Age-appropriate balance play can start very young with adult supervision. Every child differs. Skate classes at EGY begin from age four with a coach, helmet and protection.

Which activities improve balance?

Standing on one leg, walking a line, changing direction and age-appropriate core strength all help when practice stays short, safe and encouraging.

Is this phone test medical advice?

No. It is a light family game, not a medical test. Stop if you feel dizzy, and consult a qualified professional about persistent balance concerns.
