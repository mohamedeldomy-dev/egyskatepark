Safety starts first

Is Skating Safe for Your Child?

It is the first question every parent asks before a first class. The honest answer: like any active sport, skating includes the possibility of falls. What separates a harmless tumble from a worrying one comes down to three things — protection, coaching and the environment. We are strict about all three.

3 years of classes. Thousands of sessions.
Not once did we need a doctor for a class injury.

That is not luck. The first thing every child learns is how to fall safely, and full protective gear is mandatory in every class.

01

How we keep your child safe

01

Full protection is mandatory

Every child wears a helmet plus knee, elbow and wrist guards in class. It is included in the price, and no class starts without it.

02

A coach in every class

Children are never left to figure it out alone. Before teaching the right move, the coach teaches them how to fall safely.

03

A space designed for learning

The park is divided into zones, with beginners learning in a dedicated area away from advanced riders.

04

Step-by-step progression

No child moves to a harder skill until they have mastered the one before it. Our coaching follows five clear levels.

Advice for the first class

  1. 01

    Dress your child in long trousers and a comfortable T-shirt.

  2. 02

    Instead of saying, “Be careful, you’ll fall,” say, “If you fall, get back up.”

  3. 03

    Watch from a little distance at Egy Bar — children often learn faster when they do not feel closely observed.

03

Questions & Answers

My child is very nervous. Can they still learn?

Yes. After a few classes, some of our most confident students are the children who were most nervous at first. Coaches work at each child’s pace, and confidence grows through small wins.

What age can a child start?

From age 4 with roller skating — balance develops quickly at this age.

What happens if an injury occurs?

The team is trained in first aid, and a fully stocked first-aid kit is available on site.
