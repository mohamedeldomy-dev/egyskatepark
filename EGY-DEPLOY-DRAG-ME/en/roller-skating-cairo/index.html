Roller skating · Patinage

Balance, fitness and the feeling of flow.

Many Egyptians search for patinage; at Egy Skatepark it means learning roller skating properly on real concrete. Complete beginners start with stance, balance, stopping and safe falling before moving into turns and cleaner movement.

  • Children from age four
  • Teenagers and adults welcome
