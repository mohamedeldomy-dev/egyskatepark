Questions & Answers

What age can my child start roller skating?

From age 4. We also run classes for adults.

Do I need to buy skates before the class?

No. Roller skates, a helmet and full protective gear are included in the class price.

Is roller skating or skateboarding better for my child?

Roller skating is often easier at the beginning and can build balance quickly. The coach can help you choose during the first session.

Is roller skating safe?

With full protection — helmet, knee, elbow and wrist guards — plus a coach, the risk of injury is very low.
