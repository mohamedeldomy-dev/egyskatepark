Ride at your pace

One ticket gives one rider the whole park for the day.

The day pass is for independent skateboard and roller riders who want time to practise, meet their crew or discover a new line. Come with your own setup or rent a board or skates, a helmet and full protection directly from the park.

  • Beginners and experienced riders welcome
  • Real concrete bowls, banks and street lines
  • Family seating keeps the park in view
  • Egy Bar, shop and padel at the destination
Class or day pass?

Choose coaching when you need guidance; choose the pass when you want free riding.

A class includes a coach and training equipment. The day pass gives independent access without a coach. First-time riders who need help with balance, stopping or safe falling should begin with a class.

Questions before your visit.

How much is the day pass?+

The all-day park ticket is 150 EGP for one rider.

Can I arrive without equipment?+

Yes. Skateboards, roller skates, helmets and full protection are available to rent at the park.

What are the opening hours?+

The park is open daily from 2 PM to 1 AM. Confirm holiday changes on WhatsApp before travelling.
