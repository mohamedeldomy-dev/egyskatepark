A full family destination

The riders move. The whole family enjoys the day.

Children can take a class or ride independently with a day pass while parents stay close from park-view seating. Add drinks or dessert from Egy Bar, browse the skate shop, or arrange a separate padel booking through the padel application.

  • Classes with equipment included
  • 150 EGP all-day park entry
  • Shop and independent-rider rentals
  • Family seating, Egy Bar and padel
First visit made simple

Start with the rider, then build the rest of the day.

Choose a class for a first-time rider who needs coaching or a day pass for someone ready to ride independently. The team can help you combine the visit with seating, refreshments, shopping or a separately booked padel match.

Questions before your visit.

Can parents see the park?+

Yes. Family seating is positioned so parents can keep the skatepark in view.

Is there food or drink?+

Egy Bar offers hot and cold drinks, juices, shakes and selected desserts.

Is padel included with skatepark entry?+

No. Padel is booked separately through the padel application.
