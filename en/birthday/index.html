Questions & Answers

What happens if the children have never skated before?

Our coaches take them through it step by step. Most children are standing on roller skates within the first 15 minutes.

Can we bring our own cake?

Yes. Bring your cake, while drinks and desserts are ordered from Egy Bar.

What ages is the party suitable for?

Ages 4 to 16. We organise the children by age.

What if we have fewer than 10 children?

The package is charged at the 10-child minimum: 3,500 EGP.
