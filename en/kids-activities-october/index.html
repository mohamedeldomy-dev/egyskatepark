Real Places in the Area

  1. 01

    Egy Skatepark — Waslet Dahshur

    Egypt's first professional skatepark, with roller skating and skateboard classes from age 4, 150 EGP all-day entry and a café where parents can watch. Best suited to ages 4 to 16.

  2. 02

    Ski Egypt — Mall of Egypt

    Real snow, skiing and penguins. A distinctive, higher-priced experience that works well for special occasions.

  3. 03

    Dream Park — 6th of October

    A classic amusement park with many rides. Plan for a full day, particularly with older children.

  4. 04

    Play areas at Mall of Arabia and Mall of Egypt

    A quick, air-conditioned option for hot days or shorter outings.

Questions & Answers

What is the best day out for a 4–6-year-old in October?

A supervised active experience, such as a beginner roller skating class. Children at this age benefit from moving and learning under guidance, rather than only watching.

Are there places open late at night in 6th of October?

Yes. Egy Skatepark is open daily until 1 AM. In summer, the most comfortable time is after sunset.

What is an affordable family day out in October?

Skatepark entry is 150 EGP per child for the full day, while accompanying parents can watch and sit free of charge.
