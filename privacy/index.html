Privacy

Your request stays in your hands.

The current Egy Skatepark website does not store the personal information entered in the skate-class request form. The form prepares a WhatsApp message on your device. Information is sent only when you choose to continue to WhatsApp and send that message.

Information you choose to send

A class request can include a name, WhatsApp number, rider age, experience, preferred date and time window, and optional coaching notes. WhatsApp handles the message after you leave this website.

Device-local experience data

The daily game can save visit dates and a best score in local storage on the same device. This information is not transmitted to Egy Skatepark by the current website and can be removed by clearing the website data in your browser.

Analytics and advertising

This package does not include Google Analytics, advertising pixels or other marketing tracking code. The deployment team manages measurement separately and must update this notice if their implementation requires additional disclosure or consent.

Contact

For a privacy question, contact Egy Skatepark through the official WhatsApp number shown on the website.
