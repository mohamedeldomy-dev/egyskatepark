A plan for parents

They find their skate line. You find your match.

Padel gives parents and friends another way to enjoy the destination during a family visit. Court booking, duration and pricing are separate from Egy Skatepark entry and are handled through the padel application.

  • Separate court booking
  • Combine with a skate class or day pass
  • Family seating and Egy Bar nearby
  • Confirm current court details before travelling
Build one destination day

Skate, sit, play and stay—without moving the family between venues.

Choose the rider's skate plan first, then arrange padel separately for the adults. The skatepark team can explain how the experiences fit together, while the court booking remains inside the padel application.

PLAN WITH CONFIDENCE

Questions before your visit.

Is padel part of the skatepark ticket?+

No. Padel court booking and payment are separate from skatepark entry.

Where do I book?+

Use the padel application for the current court schedule, duration and price.

Can we combine padel with a class?+

Yes. Families can coordinate a skate class or day pass with a separately booked padel match.

Your next step

Tell the team your preferred day and time. They will confirm current court availability, duration and price separately from the skatepark visit.

Current availability and final details are confirmed by the Egy Skatepark team on WhatsApp before you travel.
